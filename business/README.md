

# Campus Business Startup Application

校园商业创业申报

Trial Version, Digital Technologies, 2026.10.23

<br>
<br>

## I. Workflow | 工作流程:

<b> ★ Step 1. Send the “Required Information” (one paragraph) together with “Supporting Documents” (in a ZIP or RAR file) to [dt@hibiuh.edu.cn](mailto:dt@hibiuh.edu.cn) </b>

★ 第 1 步：将 “必填信息”（一段文字）连同 “佐证材料”（打包为 ZIP 或 RAR 压缩包）发送至邮箱 [dt@hibiuh.edu.cn](mailto:dt@hibiuh.edu.cn) </b>

<br>

Step 2. First-round check: Professor Assistant will review the information, and discuss with the Program Lead. (Approved or Rejected)

第 2 步：首轮审核：专业助理核查材料信息，并与专业负责人商议。（通过 / 驳回）

<br>

<b> ★ Step 3. A PUBLIC PRESENTATION & DEFENCE (online or offline) will be arranged by the Professor Assistant and Program Lead, who will also notify students and faculty members to attend. </b>

★ 第 3 步：由专业助理和专业负责人安排公开答辩（线上或线下形式），同时通知师生到场参与。

<br>

Step 4. Second-round check: Professor Assistant share the “Required Information”(one paragraph) in Faculty Committee Chat Group.

第 4 步：二轮复核：助教将 “必填信息”（一段文字）发到系务委员会工作群。

<br>

Step 5. Final Decision: Approved or Rejected

第 5 步：最终结果：通过 / 驳回

<br>
<br>
<br>


## II. Required Information (one paragraph) (English or English & Chinese) | 必填信息（一段话）（英文 或者 英文+中文）:

1. | Your Name & brief introduction of the business | 你的姓名，简要的一句话介绍你的商业活动 
2. | Internal participants list (please indicate whether they are students or teachers) | 校内参与者名单（注明每一位老师还是学生）
3. | External participants list (please indicate whether they are your family members) | 校外参与者名单（注明每一位是否为你的家庭成员）
4. | Required Resource from BiUH (e.g., place, permission, funding, supervisor, etc.) | 从学校需求的资源（空间、许可、资助、指导老师等）
5. | Business Stage (No company established or partner contacted, Founded own company, Affiliated with an established external company) | 商业阶段（还未成立公司或联系合作公司，已自己创立公司，依托外部成熟公司）

<br>
<br>
<br>

<b> Demo ｜ 样例: </b>

Name: Li Ming. My business is a campus second-hand book sharing and recycling service for BiUH students. Internal participants: Li Ming (student), Wang Yu (student), Zhang San (teacher). External participants: Li Hua (family member), Zhao Qian (non-family member). Resources requested from BiUH: a temporary booth space, permission for on-campus promotion. Business stage: No company established or partner contacted.

姓名：李明。我的项目是面向比勒费尔德海南应用科学大学学生的校园二手图书共享回收服务。校内参与者：李明（学生）、王宇（学生）、张三（教师）。校外参与者：李华（家庭成员）、赵倩（非家庭成员）。向 BiUH 申请资源：临时摊位场地、校内宣传许可。商业阶段：还未成立公司或联系合作公司。

<br>
<br>
<br>

## III. Supporting Documents (one ZIP or RAR file) | 佐证材料（一个ZIP或者RAR压缩文件）: 

<br>

1. | Presentation slides | 路演 PPT
2. | Business plan (no template provided; prepare a brief plan) | 商业计划书（没有样例，请准备一个简单的计划）
3. | Team information sheet (must include phone number of each member; contact information will only be used for this review and will not be distributed externally.) | 团队成员信息(必须包含每一个成员的手机号; 联系方式仅本次评审使用，不对外分发)
